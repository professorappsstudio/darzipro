Features

Everything a tailor needs, nothing he doesn't.

Built with working darzis in mind: quick to learn, fast to use at the counter, clear to read.

Measurements, your way

Number, text, dropdown or tick box. Add collar styles, pocket types or any field your shop uses and change it any time.

Clear order workflow

Pending, processing, completed, then delivered. One obvious button for the next step, so nothing gets missed.

Payments & balance

Take an advance, record payments and always see what is still due. The app never lets you over-collect.

Partial deliveries

Hand over two suits now and the third later. Plus and minus per garment, and the amount is checked for you.

Print the bill

Print an order slip at any stage, with or without measurements. After the last delivery the app offers to print.

Monthly tracking

Flip through months on the dashboard and see orders, billed, collected and outstanding amounts at a glance.

English & Urdu

Switch the whole app to Urdu with a readable Nastaliq font, including right-to-left layout.

Light & dark

A calm, easy-on-the-eyes design that works in a bright shop or late at night.

Private by design

Customer and order data is stored on your own phone. No account on our servers, no ads, no tracking.

Order workflow

From the first measurement to the final delivery.

Every order moves forward one clear step at a time, and your records stay safe.

Pending

Order placed

Pick the customer, add garments, set a delivery date and take an advance.

Processing

Stitching starts

One tap on "Start processing" when work begins on the order.

Completed

Ready for pickup

Mark it completed when the garments are finished. Delivery unlocks now.

Delivered

Handed over

Deliver everything, or only some garments. Collect the exact balance on the last one.

Delivered and partly delivered orders are locked and cannot be deleted, so your books always add up.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Darzi Pro free?

Yes. You can download and use Darzi Pro for free.

Where is my data stored?

On your own phone. Your customers, measurements and orders are not uploaded to our servers. Because of that, uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them, so please keep your phone safe. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for your daily work. The app only needs a connection to download its fonts the first time you open it.

Can I print bills?

Yes. Open any order and tap Print. You can send it to a printer or save it as a PDF, and choose whether to include measurements.

Is there an iPhone version?

The App Store version is coming soon. For now, Darzi Pro is available on Google Play for Android phones.

How do I contact you?

Write to support@yourdomain.com and we will get back to you.
