1. Who we are

Darzi Pro (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile app for tailors, published by Professor Studio. It helps you manage customers, measurements, orders, payments and bills for your tailoring shop. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

2. Information the app handles

Everything below is entered by you and is saved locally on your device. We cannot see it.

InformationExamples
Account detailsYour name, email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash on your phone).
Shop detailsShop name, owner name, phone number, address, currency and price list.
Customer detailsCustomer names, phone numbers, addresses, notes and their measurements.
Orders and paymentsGarments, quantities, prices, delivery dates, payments, delivery records and order notes.
PreferencesLanguage, light or dark theme and whether you have seen the intro.

The customer information you store belongs to you. You are responsible for handling it in line with the laws that apply to your business.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not upload your shop, customer or order data to our servers.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or device identifiers for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics and crash-tracking tools inside the app.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with anyone.

4. How the information is used

The information you enter is used only to run the features you ask for: showing your customers and orders, calculating totals and balances, tracking monthly figures on the dashboard, and preparing bills for printing. It is never used for profiling or marketing.

5. Third-party services

Google Fonts

To display text in the correct typefaces (Inter, Fraunces and Noto Nastaliq Urdu), the app downloads font files from Google Fonts when needed. Like any internet request, this lets Google receive technical information such as your IP address. No personal information from the app is sent. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Printing and sharing

When you tap Print, the bill is created on your phone and handed to your device's own print service. If you choose a cloud or network printer, or save or share a PDF, that service or app receives the bill and handles it under its own policy. We do not receive it.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes your download and device information under its own terms and policy. We do not control this.

6. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it on an unmodified device.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text. Only a one-way hash is kept on your device.
  • Because everything is stored only on your phone, there is no backup on our side. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data or losing the phone can permanently remove your records. Keep your phone secure and protected with a screen lock.

No method of storage is completely secure, but keeping data on your device means we are never in a position to lose or leak it from a server.

7. Your choices and control

  • Access and edit: you can view and change your customers, measurements and orders inside the app at any time.
  • Delete: you can delete customers, and orders that have not yet been delivered. To keep your sales records accurate, delivered and partly delivered orders cannot be deleted inside the app.
  • Erase everything: uninstall the app or choose Clear storage for Darzi Pro in your phone's app settings to remove all data.
  • Log out: you can log out from Settings at any time.

Since we do not hold your data, requests to access or delete it are handled by you directly in the app. If you need help, contact us below.

8. Children's privacy

Darzi Pro is a business tool for tailors and shop staff and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the app gains new features. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and for important changes we will make a clear notice in the app or on the store listing.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@yourdomain.com.
